# Home banner artwork

Five original images generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and compressed to WebP for the website. These are decorative category illustrations, not photographs of specific products sold by Athidi Foods. The existing menu item photographs are unchanged.

Saved files: `public/assets/banners/lunch.webp`, `biryani.webp`, `sweets.webp`, `bakery.webp`, `dry-fruits.webp`.

Each generation used this prompt, substituting the corresponding subject below:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: food photograph for Athidi Foods category banner. [SUBJECT] Premium appetizing editorial food photography, warm natural daylight, soft ivory cream tabletop, subtle warm shadows, overhead three-quarter view, food centered and fully visible with breathing room. Landscape 3:2 composition. Colour palette warm cream, rich food tones and tiny dark green herb accents. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no meat. This is a standalone website asset, save a local image file.

- Lunch: A beautiful Indian vegetarian lunch thali: rice, chapati, dal, vegetable curry, curd and pickle in small steel bowls.
- Biryani: A generous copper bowl of fragrant vegetarian biryani alongside a smaller bowl of rich paneer curry.
- Sweets: An elegant brass plate of Indian sweets: golden laddus, diamond-shaped kaju katli and glossy gulab jamun.
- Bakery: An inviting arrangement of vegetarian bakery treats: a small frosted cake, bread rolls and golden cookies.
- Dry fruits: Small rustic bowls filled with almonds, cashews, pistachios, raisins and dates.
